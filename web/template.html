Mouse and keys
    
  • Left click: draw / select. Right-drag: move around. Wheel: zoom. Right click: cancel / finish.
    • 
    
  • V select, L line, R rect, C circle, A arc, U curve, T trim, E extend, B break, F fillet, H chamfer, M move, Y copy, O rotate, K scale, G stretch, I mirror, P parallel, D measure, S cut start, W freehand, X delete point.
    • 
    
  • Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo and redo. Delete removes the selection. Shift keeps lines straight. F1 opens this window.
    • 
    
  • Type a number then Enter for exact sizes: 4<90 is 4 long pointing up, 3,2 is 3 across and 2 up.

  
Your drawing saves in this browser automatically. Use Save project to keep a file.
